Design direction

Product: Talha Bhatti video editing and color grading portfolio
Character: Cinematic, measured, personal
World: Charcoal editing suite with warm gold accents
Moments: Actual films and poster frames
Signature: Timecodes beside films

Applied the nine unslop passes: consistent spacing, quiet colors, one icon family, original copy, restrained typography, a split hero, simpler details, real video imagery and purposeful motion. Existing projects and their order are retained after the five supplied clips. Unsupported testimonials and tool proficiency percentages were removed. The comparison is explicitly illustrative.

No generated imagery was needed. Posters were extracted from the supplied videos. New copy was written for this project, without copying external prose. Dashes were removed from visible copy while technical identifiers were preserved.
